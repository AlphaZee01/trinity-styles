Production load

k6 against the production server on http://127.0.0.1:3001. The warm-up opened the home page, the shop, every product, the bag, and login once, and those requests are left out. A page counts as rendered only when the response contains its real text. Orders and virtual try-on were not used. This is the document response, not a full browser.

The home page median was 688 ms for one shopper and 900 ms for fifty.
